GEARWORKS

A tiny block-machine builder — stick, power, shove, and drag your way to moving contraptions.

Color: Mover
Sandbox Select a level below to play, or stay in Sandbox

HOW TO PLAY

Goal: build machines that move on their own.

Blocks:

  • Dynamo — colored power source. Machines touching it and matching its color run. Also recharges adjacent batteries.
  • Battery — temporary power; drains 1 charge/tick (shows %). Charge it at a dynamo, then take it with you.
  • Mover — drags itself + sticky-glued friends one cell per tick.
  • Conveyor — nudges the block in front. Pusher — shoves the whole line ahead.
  • Sticky — glues neighbors into one moving group. Wire — carries color power from afar. Wall — blocks.

Controls: click to place, click a placed mover/conveyor/pusher to rotate, right-click erase, Space play/pause, Ctrl+scroll zoom, Copy/Paste moves your whole machine.

Colors: pick Yellow/Red/Blue to paint new + clicked blocks. A red mover only runs on red power.

HOW IT WORKS

Place blocks on the grid and press Space to run. Dynamo is the power source — any Mover, Conveyor, or Pusher touching a Dynamo of the same color activates. Sticky glues neighbors into one moving group. Batteries hold portable charge — recharge them next to a Dynamo, then glue them into a moving machine. Wall blocks movement, Wire transmits power color across the map.

Click a placed Mover/Conveyor/Pusher to rotate it • Right-click to erase • Ctrl+scroll to zoom • Copy/Paste duplicates your whole machine.

LEVELS

Beat the goal by giving a machine power that matches its color. Sandbox is free play.
